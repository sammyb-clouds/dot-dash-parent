How it works

Simple on purposeA small screen, two buttons and a buzzer. No camera, no microphone, no web browser and no location tracking.
You're the baseYou pair your Dot Dash in the Dot Dash app, choose who it can message, and can see those messages in the Log.
Learning while playingMorse practice, games, timers and points turn messaging into a skill you build over time.

About Dot Dash

Dot Dash designs and builds the device, the Dot Dash app, and the messaging service that connects them. Every message a Dot Dash sends or receives is delivered by Dot Dash's own servers, which also handle software updates, contact approvals and the Log.

SMS messaging (optional)

The Dot Dash service can deliver a Dot Dash's messages to a mobile phone by SMS, so someone without a Dot Dash, like a grandparent, can message it from their phone. This is optional. Nobody has to receive SMS messages to use Dot Dash. Because Dot Dash is often used by children, only the account holder can approve a phone contact.

How to join: once the account holder has added your number in the Dot Dash app, send START to (866) 786-1245.

By sending START, you agree to receive SMS messages from Dot Dash: a confirmation, then the messages sent to you from that Dot Dash. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel. See our Terms and Privacy Policy.

  • We never message a number that hasn't sent START. See the full opt-in flow.

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Mobile numbers are stored encrypted and are never shared or sold for marketing.
