How they work, how to sign up, and how to stop them.

SMS notifications (optional)

Someone without a Dot Dash, like a grandparent, can message one from our chat page using a private link the account holder sends them. On that page they can choose to get an SMS notification when a new message is waiting. Notifications only say that a message is waiting and link back to the chat. They never contain what anyone wrote. This is optional. Nobody has to receive SMS messages to use Dot Dash.

How to sign up: open your chat link, enter your own mobile number, and tick the box:

"Yes, send me SMS notifications from Dot Dash when I have a new message. Each one links back to this chat; message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel." See our Terms and Privacy Policy.

  • We never message a number that wasn't signed up this way. See the full opt-in flow.
  • You get one confirmation, then a notification only when a message is waiting: at most one until you've looked at the chat again. For example: Dot Dash: you have a new message from Maya's Dot Dash. Open it here: https://dotdashdevice.com/c/
  • Notifications come from (866) 786-1245. Reply STOP at any time to stop, or HELP for help. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Mobile numbers are stored encrypted and are never shared or sold for marketing. Turning notifications off deletes your number.
