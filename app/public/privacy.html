Dot Dash Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 September 2026

Dot Dash is a Morse-code messenger for children. The physical device is used by a child; this app is used by their parent or guardian to set the device up and to exchange messages with it. This policy covers both.

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it with advertisers, and there is no advertising or third-party analytics in the app or on the device.

What we collect

Account information. An email address and password, used only to sign the parent in. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.

Names and PINs you choose. A parent picks a short display name and a four-digit PIN for themselves and for each child — for example MOM0101 or ROBIN0614. These are nicknames chosen by the parent, not legal names, and they act as the addresses messages are sent to. We recommend a first name or a nickname rather than a full name.

Messages. Message text, the sender, and the time it was sent. Messages are drawn from a short list of phrases a parent approves, plus free Morse text tapped on the device. The most recent 200 messages per account are stored so the app can show history and so a message is not lost while a phone is asleep; older messages are deleted automatically.

Device information. The hardware identifier of each paired device, its approved phrase list, and its list of approved contacts.

Wi-Fi networks you save for a device. If you use the Wi-Fi settings in the app, the network names and passwords you enter are stored in your account so the device can be given them, and are obscured when sent to the device. They are used for nothing else. If you would rather they were not stored, set the device's Wi-Fi using its own setup portal instead.

Notification tokens. If you turn notifications on, an identifier for your phone, so alerts can be delivered to it. Turning notifications off deletes it.

What we do not collect

  • No location data, from the app or the device.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics or behavioural profiling.
  • The device has no microphone and no camera.

Children

Children do not have accounts, do not sign in, and never enter personal information. Everything about a child's device — its name, its PIN, who it may talk to, and what phrases it may send — is created and controlled by their parent or guardian in this app. A child interacts only with the hardware.

A child's device can exchange messages only with contacts a parent has approved. Requests to add a new contact are sent to the parent for approval and are not acted on until the parent approves them.

Where the data goes

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging) stores accounts, device settings and recent messages, and delivers notifications.
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications to iPhones.
  • Our own message broker, on a server we operate, passes messages between a device and the app. Traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS). Messages pass through and are not archived there.

These providers process data on our behalf so the product can work. Nobody receives this data for their own purposes.

Keeping it

Account details and device settings are kept while the account exists. Messages are kept until they fall outside the most recent 200 for the account, and are then deleted automatically.

Deleting it

You can delete your account from Settings → Delete account in the app. This removes your account, your devices, their settings and your stored messages. It cannot be undone.

You can also write to us and ask what is held about your account, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted.

Security

Traffic between the app, the device and our server is encrypted in transit. Account data is protected by access rules that limit each account to its own records. No system is perfect, and we will tell affected users if we ever become aware of a breach involving their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
