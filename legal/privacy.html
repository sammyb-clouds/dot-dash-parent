Dot Dash Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026 · Dot Dash is operated by Samuel Posner, a sole proprietor, doing business as Dot Dash.

The short version. Dot Dash is a messaging device for children, set up and controlled by a parent. We collect only what the device and the parent app need to work. We don't sell personal information, show ads, or use anyone's information for marketing. A parent can see their child's messages, remove contacts, and delete everything at any time.

1. Who this covers

This policy covers the Dot Dash device, the Dot Dash parent app (on the web at app.dotdashdevice.com and on iOS), and Dot Dash's SMS messaging feature. It describes information about three groups of people:

  • Parents, who create the account and pair the device.
  • Children, who use the device.
  • Phone contacts, meaning adults a parent approves, such as a grandparent, who exchange SMS messages with a child's device.

2. Children's privacy

Dot Dash is designed for children, including children under 13, and we follow the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • A parent sets everything up. A child can't create an account. The parent creates the account, pairs the device, and chooses the child's screen name. The parent decides who the child can message, and can change that at any time.
  • What we collect from or about a child: a screen name and a four-digit birthday code chosen by the parent (month and day only, no year), which together form the child's Dot Dash ID; the messages the child sends and receives; and activity on the device, such as game scores, points and timers.
  • Parental review. The parent app shows the parent the messages their child sends and receives.
  • Parental rights. A parent can review their child's information, stop its further collection, and have it deleted. They can do this in the app (unpair the device or delete the account), or by contacting us at support@dotdashdevice.com.
  • We don't require a child to share more information than the activity needs, and we don't use children's information for advertising or marketing.

4. How we use information

Only to provide and improve Dot Dash: delivering messages, pairing and updating devices, notifying parents, keeping the service secure, and answering support requests. We never sell or rent personal information, and we never use it for advertising or marketing.

5. SMS messaging with phone contacts

  • SMS messaging is optional. No one has to receive SMS messages to use Dot Dash: the device, the app and messaging between Dot Dash devices all work without it. Opting in to SMS is a separate choice that only the person receiving the messages can make.
  • A parent can approve a phone contact for their child's device by entering the contact's name and mobile number in the app.
  • We never message a phone number first. The contact opts in themselves by sending the keyword START to the Dot Dash number. Until they do, nothing is sent to them, and nothing they send reaches the child.
  • After opting in, the contact and the child's device can exchange messages. Messages from the child are sent to the contact as SMS messages, and the contact's replies are shown on the child's device. The parent can see these messages in the app.
  • The contact can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, and HELP for help. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Mobile numbers are stored encrypted and are never shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party, except the SMS provider that carries the messages (section 6).
  • Our servers don't keep a copy of SMS message content. Messages pass through to the device or to the contact's phone.
  • When a parent removes a contact, their number is deleted. We keep a keyed, one-way fingerprint of the number together with the Dot Dash number it used. That record can't be turned back into the phone number without a secret key held only on our server. It exists only so that Dot Dash number is never later connected to a different child's device. A contact who never sends START is deleted after 14 days.

5A. Contacts by link

  • Instead of a phone number, a parent can give an adult a private link to a chat page with their child's device. No phone number, account or app is needed. The link works only on the first device that connects with it.
  • Messages between the link contact and the device are kept on our server so the chat page can show them: at most the 50 most recent, and none older than 30 days.
  • If the link contact turns on notifications, we store the notification address their browser gives us, and use it only to tell them about new messages from the device.
  • The link contact can choose Leave this chat at any time, which ends the connection and deletes the chat history and notification address. A parent can remove a link contact, or replace their link, at any time; removing them deletes everything about them. A link that's never opened is deleted after 14 days, and a contact who left is deleted after 30 days.

6. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that run Dot Dash, and only for that purpose:

  • Google Firebase: account sign-in, the database, and push notifications.
  • Apple: push notifications on iPhone.
  • DigitalOcean: the server that relays messages between devices and runs the parent app.
  • Twilio: sending and receiving SMS messages for phone contacts. Twilio receives the contact's number and the message content to deliver them.
  • Browser notification services (such as Apple, Google or Mozilla, depending on the link contact's browser): delivering notifications to link contacts who turn them on. Notifications are encrypted so only the contact's browser can read them.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of a child or anyone else. We don't share information with anyone else. If Dot Dash is ever sold or transferred, this policy will continue to apply to the information collected under it.

7. How long we keep information

  • Messages pass through our server and are removed from it once the device or the parent app has received them. The device keeps its 10 most recent messages. The parent app's history keeps the 200 most recent messages to the parent and the 200 most recent entries in the activity feed; older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Wi-Fi passwords are kept only until the device confirms it has the network.
  • Device and child information is kept while the device is paired. Unpairing removes the device from the parent's account.
  • Phone contacts: as described in section 5. Link contacts: as described in section 5A.
  • Everything else is kept until the parent deletes their account.

8. Deleting your information

Delete My Account in the parent app's Settings unpairs every device and erases the account's devices, messages, activity feed, push tokens, profile, Dot Dash IDs and phone contacts, then closes the account. You can also ask us to delete your information by emailing support@dotdashdevice.com. We'll confirm when it's done. Copies in encrypted backups kept by our providers expire on their normal schedule.

9. Security

All connections to Dot Dash are encrypted. Each device is moving to its own login, so a device can reach only its own messages. Phone numbers are encrypted at rest. Access to our servers is limited to the operator. No system is perfectly secure, and if we learn of a breach that affects your information, we'll tell you.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how we collect, use or share children's information, we'll notify parents and, where the law requires it, ask for their consent again before the change applies. The effective date at the top always shows the latest version.
