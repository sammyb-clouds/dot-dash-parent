Dot Dash: how someone opts in to SMS notifications

Dot Dash is a Morse-code messaging device. The account holder can invite someone, like a grandparent, to chat with their Dot Dash on our web page using a private link. SMS notifications are optional, and the account holder never enters anyone's phone number. Consent comes only from the person themselves, on our web form, before anything is sent. Notifications only say a message is waiting and link back to the chat. They never contain message content.
